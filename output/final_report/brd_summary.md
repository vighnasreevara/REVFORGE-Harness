## What this system does

The Portfolio Management System maintains investment portfolios and their supporting batch and online services. Batch programs add, update, delete and list portfolio master records, validate portfolio transactions (buy, sell, transfer, fee), load transaction history into the DB2 database, and produce position, audit, statistics and return-code reports. A batch-control layer sequences jobs, checks their dependencies, records checkpoints and decides whether a failed job is restarted, bypassed or terminated. Online (CICS) programs let a user look up a portfolio's positions and an account's transaction history, behind a security check that validates the user, checks authorisation and records access. The business domain is inferred from program narratives, file names and field names.
